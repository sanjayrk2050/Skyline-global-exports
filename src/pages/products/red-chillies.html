The Origin Story

From the Heart of
the Guntur Belt.

The Guntur region is globally renowned for its unique soil composition and climatic conditions, which impart a distinct potency and color to the chillies grown here.

We partner with audited cooperatives to ensure a fair-trade ecosystem. Our procurement process involves multi-stage sampling at the farmgate, followed by specialized drying and cleaning processes that eliminate impurities while preserving the essential oils.

By maintaining a direct link between the grower and the exporter, we guarantee total traceability and a consistent grade across every container.
